METAMATICS

About the project

This is an educational PC game that teaches math to children from 1st to 6th grade. The brand combines a playful, vibrant style with magical elements: a cape that symbolizes adventure and discovery. I was in charge of the identity, the interface redesign (with a grid and behavior rules for each container) and the intro animation, made in Unity.

My contribution

  • Design of the brand identity and brand materials.
  • UI redesign on a 1920 px grid, with 25, 50 and 100 px margins for the scroll.
  • Functional definition: text containers with a minimum (20 pt with an image) and maximum (50 pt) size to avoid scrolling, and image containers with a scroll bar for tall images.
  • Overlay layer under the main elements and over the secondary ones; on the character and round selection, a dark violet at 50% transparency.
  • Character selection with characters identified by name, separate buttons for round and character, and a new confirm button to play.
  • Animation of the game's introduction in Unity, plus wireframes, visual documentation and storyboard.
