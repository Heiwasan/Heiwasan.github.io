About the project

This is a mobile app for shopping mall visitors: Shopping Mariscal López, Mariano and Plaza Norte. It brings together promos and discounts by bank and store, gift card and gift check purchases, parking payment with saved plates, receipt upload with a tutorial and card registration, plus profile, sign-up and notifications. Each mall has its own version: switching malls changes the brand, color and screens.
