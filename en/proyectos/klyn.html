About the project

This is a Paraguayan platform to buy, sell, deposit and send digital assets in guaraníes or dollars. It has balances, a trading desk with chat and a transaction history. Sign-up validates identity with an ID document and a liveness check, and it also reviews the wallet's security.

My contribution

  • Design of the complete product on mobile, web and tablet.
  • Analysis of sign-up and identity verification: ID document, liveness check and wallet security review.
  • Design of the buy and sell operations, the trading desk, deposits, transfers and history.
  • Built a custom design system, with financial, crypto and identity-verification components, based on the client's brand manual.
  • Adapted the product to the Paraguayan version, with settlement in guaraníes and dollars.
  • Built the design with prompts in Claude, plus manual refinements for a more polished finish.
