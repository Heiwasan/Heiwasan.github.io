ROMANCE GO

ROMANCE GO

About the project

This is a mobile dating and friendship game with customizable avatars, encounters with characters (NPCs), mini-games, a selfie mode, a memory album and an in-game social network called Cupigram. I was in charge of the identity and the complete design of the game, and I also worked as QA on Android and iOS devices.

My contribution

  • Design lead for the project: brand identity and mobile app design.
  • User-centered design principles and definition of the complete design system.
  • Design of the assets and screens, with low and high fidelity mockups.
  • UX research: competitor analysis and comparison.
  • Graphic documentation of the behavior of each screen and button (gestures, selfie camera, flash, sharing).
  • Design of the initial tutorial (FTUE) in the dressing room and the map, and of the push and in-game notification system, with its prioritization.
  • Design of icons, buttons and pop-ups, and the brand manual.
  • Worked with the art and development teams, and made improvements based on beta tester feedback.
  • QA role during development: testing the game on a range of Android and iOS mobile devices.
