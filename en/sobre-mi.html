About me

How I work

I am a UX/UI designer working at the intersection of research, aesthetics and product. I believe an elegant interface also has to be clear, accessible and consistent.

I combine systems thinking with a strong care for typographic and visual detail.

  • Requirements gathering and functional analysis

    Surveys, interviews and flows.

  • Interface design

    Web, mobile and responsive with clear hierarchy and editorial composition.

  • Design Systems

    Tokens, components and documentation ready for production.

  • Prototyping and testing

    Interactive flows and validation with real users.
